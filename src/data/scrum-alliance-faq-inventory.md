## Certified ScrumMaster®: FAQ SA

**Q:** How do I become a Certified ScrumMaster?

**A:** If you would like to become a Certified ScrumMaster® (CSM®), you will need to attend a CSM course taught by an approved trainer. The CSM course includes 16 hours of live instruction. After the course, you will be eligible to take the Certified ScrumMaster exam. Upon successful completion of the exam, you will receive your certification. Read our FAQ Scrum Alliance list of questions.

**Q:** Can I become a Certified ScrumMaster without taking a CSM® class?

**A:** No. Successful completion of a Certified ScrumMaster course (16 hours of live instruction) is required to take the CSM exam. Passing the exam will result in becoming a Certified ScrumMaster.

**Q:** Do I take the CSM exam during class?

**A:** You must take your CSM exam within 90 days of your CSM course. Although some providers choose to administer the exam at the end of class, we at 3Back believe that students should be allowed to utilize the 90-day timeframe to study or review if they wish. Additionally, we understand that many learners will be more comfortable taking the exam in an environment of their own choosing. Therefore, we do not offer the exam in class, instead allowing each student to take it at his or her own convenience. Please note, although you have 90 days during which to take your exam, you must take the exam in its entirety during one sitting. You will not be able to stop and restart the exam.

**Q:** How and when do I take the CSM exam?

**A:** After completing your CSM course, your instructor will notify the Scrum Alliance that you are now eligible to take the exam. You will then receive an email from Scrum Alliance that includes a link to create your login credentials. The first step will be to take the exam. The exam is online and non-proctored, so you can take it at your convenience from your home or work anytime within 90 days of your course. Once the test is complete, you will be asked to accept a license agreement and setup your online profile. It typically takes 1-2 days after course completion to receive your welcome email from the Scrum Alliance. Please note, although you have 90 days during which to take your exam, you must take the exam in its entirety during one sitting. You will not be able to stop and restart the exam.

**Q:** What if I fail the CSM exam?

**A:** Although 3Back students have a pass rate of over 99%, in the event that you should need to retake the exam, a second attempt is included at no cost. Additional attempts are $25 each. Please be aware that the exam is designed and administered by the Scrum Alliance. While we have done our best to provide the most up to date information, we do suggest checking the Scrum Alliance directly for additional details or confirmation of the above.

**Q:** Do I have to pay extra for the exam?

**A:** Your initial exam fee is included in your course registration. If necessary, the cost of a second attempt at the exam is also included. Additional attempts are $25 each. Also, if you do not take the exam within 90 days of your course, you will be required to pay a $25 fee to take the exam. Please be aware that the exam is designed and administered by the Scrum Alliance. While we have done our best to provide the most up to date information, we do suggest checking the Scrum Alliance directly for additional details or confirmation of the above.

**Q:** More than 90 days has passed since my CSM course. Can I still take the exam?

**A:** Yes. However, you will be required to pay a $25 exam fee since your 90-day window has elapsed. Tell me more about the CSM exam. In order to obtain your certification following the CSM class, you will be required to pass an online exam administered by the Scrum Alliance. The exam is online and non-proctored, so you can take it from the convenience of your own home or work. You will need to take the exam within 90 days of your CSM course. It will be timeboxed to 60 minutes. You must take the exam in its entirety during one sitting. You will not be able to stop and restart the exam. The exam consists of 50 questions selected randomly from a battery of questions. A passing score requires a minimum of 37 correct answers. Please be aware that the exam is designed and administered by the Scrum Alliance. While we have done our best to provide the most up to date information, we do suggest checking the Scrum Alliance directly for additional details or confirmation of the above.

**Q:** What is the Scrum Alliance?

**A:** The Scrum Alliance is the world’s largest, oldest and most recognized Scrum certification organization. 3Back is approved to teach courses that will lead to Scrum Alliance issued certifications.

**Q:** Can I get PDUs for this class? How many?

**A:** Yes, our CSM course is worth 14 PDUs each.

**Q:** What is required for the CSM Certification renewal in 2 years?

**A:** 20 SEU®s need to be completed in 2 years time and payment fee of $100 to the Scrum Alliance must be made, before the CSM license can be renewed. Earning a new Scrum Alliance certification will automatically renew and extend all of your earned certifications and membership to demonstrate your continuing education without submitting SEUs or paying the renewal fee. See the link for more information on Claiming SEUs. You can also use 3Back Resources to read our publications and receive SEUs under Category E.

## Certified Scrum Professional® – ScrumMaster: FAQ SA

**Q:** What are the prerequisites for the CSP®-SM course?

**A:** You need to hold an Advanced Certified ScrumMaster (A-CSM) certification with the Scrum Alliance and have at least 24 months of work experience specific to the role of a ScrumMaster within the past five years.

**A:** 40 SEU®s need to be completed in 2 years time and payment fee of $250 to the Scrum Alliance must be made before the CSP-SM license can be renewed. Earning a new Scrum Alliance certification will automatically renew and extend all of your earned certifications and membership to demonstrate your continuing education without submitting SEUs or paying the renewal fee. See the links for more information on Claiming SEUs and you can use 3Back Resources to read our publications and receive SEUs under Category E.

**Q:** Can I get PDUs and Scrum Education Units® for this class? How many?

**A:** Yes, you will receive 16 PDUs upon completion of the course. Earning a new Scrum Alliance certification will automatically renew and extend all of your earned certifications and membership to demonstrate your continuing education without submitting SEU®s or paying the renewal fee.

**Q:** What are the requirements to finish my On-Demand CSP-SM certification?

**A:** Complete 14 Self-Paced Modules with assignments. Attend 4 One-Hour sessions of structured, LO-focused, instructor-led time.

**Q:** What is the format of the course like?

**A:** On-Demand is a blended approach of both instructor-led and independent, self-paced learning. As a learner, you can find your own rhythm and you choose how fast you go. You have up to 3 months to complete the course and office hours from the purchase date. The typical Module includes 20-60 minutes of reading (depending on your reading speed), and a multiple-choice quiz. After you complete these, you need to work through assignments in the module/quiz and do one or more scenario-based exercises. Each module will take about 1-3 hours of your time.

**Q:** How long does the CSP-SM On-Demand course take to complete?

**A:** You have up to 3 months to complete the course from the purchase date. The average completion time is 4-6 weeks. Fastest On-Demand: A fast pace would be two weeks for completion. You need to attend 4 hours of structured, LO-focused, instructor-led time, and we are each planning to have two available per week. Quick On-Demand: If you want to be quick, it makes sense to do it in a month. Do 3-4 modules a week, and attend a hour of structured, LO-focused, instructor-led time each week. Minimum On-Demand: You must be finished within 3 months of the purchase date (see our policy page for details).

**Q:** I completed the CSP-SM course, but my certification is not showing up on my Scrum Alliance Account. Why?

**A:** You need to make sure you update your Scrum Alliance profile with your work experience and have an Advanced Certified ScrumMaster (A-CSM) certification. In addition to needing an A-CSM certification, you need at least 24 months of professional experience as a ScrumMaster during the last five years.

**Q:** What happens if the course gets cancelled?

**A:** When you register for one of our classes, we take your commitment seriously and will do everything we can to ensure your class will run. Although we do reserve the right to cancel or reschedule courses due to unforeseen events (such as illness, family emergency, or inclement travel weather), it is extremely rare that we do so. In the event that we do find it necessary to cancel or reschedule your class, you will be given the option of rescheduling your attendance to a future class or receiving a full refund.
