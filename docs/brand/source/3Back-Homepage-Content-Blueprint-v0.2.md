## Job and audience

Establish 3Back as The Team Execution Company for executives and senior operational leaders. Explain the team-first AI-selection problem without making the homepage an exhaustive theory page. Offer distinct ways to engage. Operational Grip remains the operating lens and its subpage carries the model.

## Locked sequence

1. Hero and selection card. Eyebrow: Team execution under pressure. H1: Organizations do not outgrow teams. Supporting thesis: They lose the boundaries that focus human expertise and AI capability on outcomes the organization needs. Teamwork with AI expands what is possible and how quickly it can be explored. As possibilities become less costly to produce, the constraint shifts toward selection. Service statement: 3Back helps leaders work through a bounded execution problem and build the capability to handle the next one. Assessment note: About 7 minutes · Get your scores.
2. Operational Grip. Preserve the question about people closest to the work making local changes whose effects remain observable and correctable. A team has Operational Grip when it can act within a useful boundary, see what changed, and correct course before local failure becomes systemic failure. Four signals: Legible demand, Guided selection, Bounded failure, Visible results. They are concurrent signals, not a sequence or a guarantee.
3. Engage. One problem. Three ways to engage. Assess it / Check your operational grip. Work it / Workshop a bounded problem. Keep the capability / Build your Domain Guides program. The guides and retained domain expertise belong to the organization.
4. Why 3Back. Concise sourced credibility, with About and Reviews links. Retain approved prototype content; no fabricated metrics, testimonials, or outcomes.
5. Current Ideas. Four illustrated cards: latest Tales strip, No Head Works Alone, How 3Back Approaches Learning, and the books collection. Approved short descriptions and Operational Grip marked Coming soon. Orange divider above the unchanged shared footer.
