# 3Back Minimum Viable Brand and Design Brief
## Appendix A — Scrum Alliance Course Ecosystem Exception

**Version 0.1**  
**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Companion to:** 3Back Minimum Viable Brand and Design Brief v0.1  
**Status:** Standing rule for classic SA credential pages

---

### Purpose of this appendix

This appendix records a controlled, limited exception to the primary visual and commercial posture defined in the 3Back Minimum Viable Brand and Design Brief. It applies only to the historical Scrum Alliance credential sub-ecosystem on 3Back.com.

### Scope

Applies to the classic Scrum Alliance credential pages and their shared template pattern, beginning with:

- Certified ScrumMaster® (CSM®) — `/certified-scrummaster-training`
- Certified Scrum Product Owner® (CSPO®) and the remaining classic SA credentials that follow the same template

It does not apply to Operational Grip pages, the homepage, Training routing page, Papers, Doomscroll, Ideas, or any other primary-brand surface.

### Brand hierarchy (unchanged)

3Back remains The Team Execution Company. Operational Grip and execution diagnosis remain the center of the brand. The Scrum Alliance credential pages live in a historical sub-ecosystem. That sub-ecosystem must be excellent at selling the six classic credentials. It does not punch through the primary brand.

### Allowed commercial and visual exception

Within this sub-ecosystem only, the following deviations from pure quiet authority are authorized:

- Stronger commercial push and more assertive selling feel than the rest of the site
- Stronger, higher-contrast primary CTAs (especially private / on-site course requests)
- Greater visual weight on credential marks, inclusions, and “why 3Back” claims
- New generated imagery designed for attraction and quality (old tired assets retired for these pages)
- Goal: a visitor should feel immediate quality and think “I want this.” The page should outperform a generic certification landing page on desire and clarity of next action.

### Still required

- Remains recognizably 3Back (warm paper / charcoal base, no foreign brand system)
- No stock photography, no cartoon treatments, no loud animations or heavy effects
- No em dashes in public copy
- No elevation of training above execution work or Operational Grip
- Claims limited to what has been explicitly approved for these pages

### Approved differentiators for this ecosystem (current)

- Delivered continuously from the days of inception to the present
- 25,000+ students
- Trainer depth and three decades of history
- Study Guide, practice exams, LMS support, and authorized pass-rate language
- Primary audience: people sending teams (private); individuals not dissuaded

### Schema

Optimized Course schema is required on individual credential pages. FAQPage schema for the shared Scrum Alliance Courses FAQ is handled separately.

### Governance

This exception is deliberate and bounded. Expansion of the stronger commercial treatment beyond the classic SA credential pages requires a new explicit decision. Silence does not expand the exception.
